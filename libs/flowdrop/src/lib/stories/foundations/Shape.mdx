# Radius and shadow

Corners and shadows tell you what kind of thing you are looking at.

## Radius

Small things have small corners. In Graphite, controls (inputs, buttons) have 6px corners,
surfaces (cards, nodes, floating controls, sheets) 8px, and message bubbles 12px. Pills and round
buttons use `--fd-radius-full`.

<Story of={Shape.RadiusScale} />

## Shadow

Flat by default. A shadow means "this floats above something": a menu over a panel, a control
over the canvas, a dialog over the page. Graphite uses one soft float elevation for everything
that sits on the canvas. Use a rule (`--fd-border`) to separate areas, not a shadow.
